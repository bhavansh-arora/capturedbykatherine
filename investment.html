How do I secure my date?

Send an enquiry and you’ll receive a booking form outlining the collections. Return it signed with a $330 deposit and your date is secured. The balance is due one week before the wedding.

Will my price change?

Prices are subject to change, but once you’ve booked, your price is locked in.

What currency are your prices in?

All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD). Couples travelling from overseas can pay by international bank transfer or card, and Katherine is happy to talk through destination plans.

Can I add more hours?

Yes. Extra coverage is available at $330 per hour, and the collection finder will tell you when adding hours is better value than moving up a collection.

Do you travel?

Travel in and around Brisbane is included. Katherine has photographed weddings from Byron Bay to the Yarra Valley, so just ask about your destination.
