(05) · QuestionsGood to know

Before the big night.

What’s the difference between the two sessions?

Both run for two hours at one location. The Pre-Formal covers up to 10 couples for $750 + GST ($75 per extra couple) and includes corsage placement. The Pre-Semi is a flat $750 + GST.

What photo combinations do you take?

Individuals, couples, families and the whole group, plus the little details. Bring a list of must-have combinations and Katherine will work through it.

How do we receive the images?

Through a private online gallery that everyone in the group can access, with full printing rights. Expect 500+ edited, high-resolution images.

Can families share the cost?

Absolutely. That’s what the cost splitter above is for. One family books, everyone shares the gallery link.
