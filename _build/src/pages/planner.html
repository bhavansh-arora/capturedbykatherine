Planner’s notesFrom 16 years of wedding days

Little things that change everything.

Katherine builds a custom timeline with every couple at the venue consultation. These are the notes she always shares.

01 · Light

Follow the sun.

Queensland sunsets come early in winter. Timing portraits for the hour before sunset gives you that warm, glowing light everyone loves.

02 · Buffer

Build in breathing room.

Add 15 minutes of buffer around family photos and travel. Relaxed couples make for the most natural images.

03 · Weather

Always have a plan B.

Walk the venue together and pick a covered spot for portraits. Then if the clouds roll in, nobody panics.
