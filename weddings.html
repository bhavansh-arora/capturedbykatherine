(01) · The approachCandid · Relaxed · Thoughtful

A father’s tears. Light on lace. The glance no one else saw.

Immersed in beautiful settings, Katherine’s passion lies in capturing the raw emotion of a wedding: the simple, profound scenes that make the day unmistakably yours.

She documents both the unscripted and the beautifully considered (the gown, the flowers, the atmosphere) and weaves them into a story that feels personal, emotional and timeless.

(02) · Getting startedBefore · During · After

More than showing up with a camera.

Weddings are deeply personal, so Katherine builds a connection with you long before the big day.

Connection first.

Katherine genuinely loves the lead-up: photos of your dress, your shoes, your invitations and the little details that bring your story to life.

A walk through your venue.

A relaxed consultation on location to explore the space, shape the timeline and plan thoughtfully for every kind of weather.

On the day, all in.

The joy, the stillness, the chaos, the laughter and the dancefloor. Photographed with intention, so the moments that matter to you are never missed.

(05) · How it worksEnquiry to heirloom

Calm, clear and quietly handled.

Reservations typically fill 9 to 12 months in advance. Once you’re booked, your price is locked in, even if prices change.

Check your date

Before the wedding

Book & plan

  1. Send an enquiry and receive a booking form outlining the collections.
  2. Choose your collection and return the signed booking form.
  3. Pay the $330 deposit and your date is secured.
  4. Meet at your venue 1 to 3 months out to finalise details and the timeline.
  5. The balance is due one week before the wedding.

On your day

Relax & enjoy

Katherine’s job is to bring out the best imagery while making sure you’re comfortable at all times. She’ll do whatever it takes to keep everything running smoothly.

After the wedding

Your story, delivered

  1. A selection of preview images the following day.
  2. Your full collection, edited and high-resolution, within six weeks (sooner outside peak season).
  3. A private online gallery to download, share, choose favourites and order prints.
  4. Images licensed to you for personal use; copyright remains with Captured by Katherine.
